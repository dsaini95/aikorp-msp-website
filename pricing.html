Questions

Are Microsoft 365 licences included?

No. Plan prices cover our management and support only. You can keep buying licences from Microsoft or your current reseller, and we will manage and right-size them for you so you never pay for seats you do not use.

Is there a minimum contract?

Plans are available month to month. Annual agreements get a 10% discount. Prices for Indian customers are in rupees, with 18% GST added. Customers outside India are billed in US dollars. Onboarding projects are quoted separately as a fixed fee.

What does onboarding cost?

It depends on the size and state of your environment. After the free health check we send a fixed-fee quote, so there are no surprises.

Do you charge for migrations?

Migrations are fixed-fee projects quoted after discovery. Customers who join a managed plan get a discounted migration rate.

Do you support on-site work?

We are remote-first, which keeps prices low. On-site visits within our area are available at an hourly rate.

Are you a Microsoft Partner?

AIKorp is an independent MSP and is not a Microsoft Partner. Our team are Microsoft 365 specialists, and being independent means our advice is not shaped by sales targets.
